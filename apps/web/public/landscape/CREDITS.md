# Landscapes

The Mist photograph by Nik Iurev is bundled under the Unsplash License:
https://unsplash.com/photos/Pk1MrGNquo8

This is the same photograph used by NativePi's desktop interface, resized to
1920 × 1280 for the website. See the desktop background credits for its source:
`apps/desktop/src/renderer/assets/backgrounds/CREDITS.md`.

`mist-dither.png` is a transparent, ordered 8 × 8 Bayer dither of the photograph,
prepared at 960 × 640 and enlarged with nearest-neighbor sampling. It provides
the still presentation without JavaScript or WebGL. The OGL enhancement draws
the same kind of screen-aligned grid and changes density briefly on pointer input.

The download page uses NativePi's Dunes photograph by NIR HIMI, also bundled
under the Unsplash License: https://unsplash.com/photos/Nl8huOhdcQA

`dunes.webp` is resized to 1920 × 1080. `dunes-dither.png` is its ordered Bayer
dither, prepared at 960 × 540 and enlarged with nearest-neighbor sampling.
Its warm neutral ink is RGB (174, 170, 159). Both the live and still layers use
this distinct image; the landing page continues to use Mist.

The app views at `public/app/desktop.webp` and `public/app/phone.webp` are real
captures of the same conversation in the running desktop app and its Local Access
phone layout. The desktop's surrounding shadow was cropped; the interface was
not reconstructed or edited.
