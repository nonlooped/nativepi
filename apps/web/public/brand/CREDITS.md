# Product and platform marks

These marks identify compatibility and project provenance, not endorsement.
Their geometry is preserved; Apple and Windows use monochrome/brand-color fills
appropriate to the site's dark ground.

- Apple: the Apple mark from Simple Icons 11.15.0, distributed under CC0.
  https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.15.0/icons/apple.svg
- Windows: the Windows 11 four-pane mark from Simple Icons 11.15.0, under CC0.
  https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.15.0/icons/windows11.svg
- Linux: Tux, by Larry Ewing (created with The GIMP), Simon Budig, and Garrett
  LeSage. The vector rendering is supplied through Wikimedia Commons:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tux.svg
  Original artwork: https://isc.tamu.edu/~lewing/linux/
  Vector source: https://github.com/garrett/Tux
  Garrett LeSage's rendering is CC0. Original attribution and notice:
  These drawings are copyrighted by Larry Ewing and Simon Budig
  (penguin-variant.sk also by Anja Gerwinski); redistribution is free but must
  include this README/copyright notice.
- GitHub: GitHub's original mark, retained as inline SVG in `components/site/Marks.tsx`.
- Pi: https://pi.dev/logo-auto.svg, retained in `public/brand/pi-logo.svg` and
  inline in `components/site/Marks.tsx`.
